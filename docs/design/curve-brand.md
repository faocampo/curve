# Curve Brand Identity

## Final logo

The Curve logo is the horizontal lockup consisting of the curved **C** symbol and the `Curve` wordmark. This proposal is the final Curve logo for product surfaces, documentation, presentations, and approved external materials.

| Surface | Final asset | Approved use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light backgrounds | [curve-logo-light-v1.png](brand/curve-logo-light-v1.png) | White and very light neutral surfaces. The wordmark is Curve Ink. |
| Dark backgrounds | [curve-logo-dark-v1.png](brand/curve-logo-dark-v1.png) | Curve Night and other very dark neutral surfaces. The wordmark is white. |

## Lockup anatomy

The curved C symbol carries the product’s motion and orchestration identity. The wordmark establishes the product name at a glance. Always use the complete horizontal lockup unless an interface has a separately approved icon-only application.

| Element | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Symbol | Abstract curved C, shown to the left of the wordmark. |
| Wordmark | `Curve`, rendered exactly with an uppercase C and lowercase `urve`. |
| Lockup orientation | Horizontal only. |
| Symbol color | Curve Indigo `#4F5BFF` and Curve Violet `#6D4CFF`. |
| Light-surface wordmark | Curve Ink `#101828`. |
| Dark-surface wordmark | White `#FFFFFF`. |
| Light-surface canvas | White `#FFFFFF` or a neutral light surface with equivalent contrast. |
| Dark-surface canvas | Curve Night `#0B1020` or a neutral dark surface with equivalent contrast. |

## Usage rules

1. Select the light asset for light surfaces and the dark asset for dark surfaces.
2. Keep clear space equal to at least one-quarter of the symbol height on every side of the lockup.
3. Keep the logo at least 120 px wide in digital use; use a larger size in presentations and editorial layouts.
4. Preserve the symbol-to-wordmark spacing, proportions, and colors supplied in the approved assets.
5. Place the mark on a flat, high-contrast surface. The dark asset is the approved option for dark UI, images, and covers.

## Controlled variations

The two supplied assets are the approved color variations:

- **Light lockup:** colored symbol with Curve Ink wordmark.
- **Dark lockup:** colored symbol with white wordmark.

New treatments, including icon-only, stacked, monochrome, animated, co-branded, or campaign-specific lockups, require an explicit design decision and a new versioned asset.

## Prohibited changes

- Changing the `Curve` spelling, capitalization, type treatment, or symbol geometry.
- Recoloring the symbol or wordmark outside the two approved variants.
- Adding shadows, outlines, gradients behind the logo, taglines, badges, or enclosing containers.
- Separating the symbol and wordmark, compressing the lockup, or placing it on low-contrast imagery.

## Asset provenance

The logo proposal derives from the Curve mark selected in the [main dashboard mockup](mockups/curve-dashboard-v1.png). The two assets in `docs/design/brand/` are the canonical raster references for v1. A vector source should be created from the approved geometry before production implementation at favicon, app-icon, or print sizes.
